BODYBUILDING
TRANSFORMATION

12 WEEKS · PUSH/PULL/LEGS × 2 DAILY
FOUNDATION (WK 1–5) → RAMPING (WK 6–12)

// PROGRAM NOTES

  • Split Logic: Days 1–3 strength-focused (3–5 min rest). Days 4–6 hypertrophy (90–120 sec). Double frequency/muscle weekly.
  • RPE 10 ✕ = final set to absolute failure — always last exercise.
  • Foundation (WK 1–5): Add 2.5–5kg weekly. Pendlay Row & Smith Squat anchors.
  • Ramping (WK 6–12): Meadows Row, Super-Stretch Fly, Meadows Lateral. Full eccentric stretch.
  • Volume Ramp: Hypertrophy days +1 set/muscle/week WK 6–11. Strength days keep volume, increase load. WK 12 tapers.
  • Recovery: Day 7 mandatory rest. Protein ≥1.8g/kg, +200–300 kcal surplus.
